Listing Type & Classification

Listing Type Guide (Rental)

Exclusive Right to Lease (ExclusiveRightToLease)

The broker earns commission no matter who leases the property — even if the landlord finds the tenant themselves. This is the most common and most protective rental listing agreement.

Full RLS distribution. Full commission protection.

Exclusive Agency (ExclusiveAgency)

One broker is the exclusive agent, but if the landlord leases the property themselves (no broker involved), they do NOT owe commission. The broker only earns commission if the lease goes through the brokerage.

RLS eligible, but less commission protection for the agent.

Exclusive Right w/ Exception (ExclusiveRightWithException)

Same as Exclusive Right to Lease, except for specific named exceptions listed in the agreement (e.g., "If tenant is John Smith, no commission owed"). Commission is owed on all other leases.

RLS eligible. Document the exceptions in the listing agreement.

Co-Exclusive (CoExclusive)

Two brokers share the exclusive right. Either broker can lease the property. Commission is owed regardless of who finds the tenant. Co-brokering terms must be in writing.

RLS eligible. Both firms named on listing.

In-House (Company Private) NOT ON RLS

A company-only private listing kept exclusively within Mallan Real Estate. Not submitted to REBNY RLS, not distributed to any portal. Only visible on mallan.nyc and marketed by our agents directly.

No RLS, no IDX, no syndication. Internal use only.

Owner Opt-Out (Permissions=OwnerOptOut)

Landlord has signed Exhibit B opting out of public distribution. Listing goes to RLS but is hidden from all public channels (IDX, syndication, portals). Only visible to RLS Participants directly.

Participant Only (Permissions=Private)

Similar to Owner Opt-Out but set by the broker. Listing is on RLS but not displayed publicly. Visible only to authorized RLS Participants.

Per UCBA: Only exclusive listings are eligible for RLS. The ListingAgreement field maps to RESO standard. In-House listings are internal only and are never submitted to REBNY.

Owner Opt-Out / Participant Only Selected

Per REBNY UCBA: IDX, VOW, Syndication, Listhub, NY MLS, Realtor, and RPX will be disabled. Listing will remain visible on WWW (your website) and shared with RLS Participants only.

Owner Opt-Out form must be signed within 48 hours and kept on file.

In-House — Company Private Listing

Not submitted to REBNY RLS or any external channel. Only visible on mallan.nyc and marketed directly by Mallan agents.
