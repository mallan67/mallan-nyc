Listing Type Guide

Exclusive Right to Sell / Lease (ExclusiveRightToSell / ExclusiveRightToLease)

The broker earns commission no matter who sells/leases the property — even if the owner finds the buyer/tenant themselves. Most common and most protective listing agreement.

Full RLS distribution. Full commission protection.

Exclusive Agency (ExclusiveAgency)

One broker is the exclusive agent, but if the owner sells/leases themselves (no broker involved), they do NOT owe commission.

RLS eligible, but less commission protection for the agent.

Exclusive Right w/ Exception (ExclusiveRightWithException)

Same as Exclusive Right, except for specific named exceptions listed in the agreement. Commission owed on all other transactions.

RLS eligible. Exceptions documented in listing agreement.

Co-Exclusive (CoExclusive)

Two brokers share the exclusive right. Either can sell/lease. Commission owed regardless of who finds the buyer/tenant.

RLS eligible. Both firms named on listing.

In-House (Company) NOT ON RLS

Filters to Mallan Real Estate company-only listings. These are private listings not submitted to REBNY RLS, marketed exclusively by our agents.

Internal only — no IDX, no syndication.

RLS: Private Owner Opt-Out

Owner signed Exhibit B. Listing is on RLS but hidden from all public channels. Only visible to RLS Participants.

RLS: Private Participant Only

Broker-set restriction. Listing on RLS but not displayed publicly. Visible only to authorized RLS Participants.

Per UCBA: Only exclusive listings are eligible for RLS. ListingAgreement maps to RESO standard. In-House uses office ID filter. Owner Opt-Out/Participant Only use Permissions fields.
