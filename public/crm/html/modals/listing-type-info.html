Listing Agreement Guide

Exclusive (search box)

Selects every exclusive agreement Cotality defines: Exclusive Right To Sell, Exclusive Right To Lease, Exclusive Right With Exception and Exclusive Agency. Cotality has no single value called “Exclusive”.

Exclusive Right To Sell (ExclusiveRightToSell)

A contract giving the Broker the right to collect commission if the property is sold by anyone, including the owning, during the term of the agreement.

Exclusive Right To Lease (ExclusiveRightToLease)

A contract giving the Broker the right to collect commission if the property is leased by anyone, including the owning, during the term of the agreement.

Exclusive Right With Exception (ExclusiveRightWithException)

A contract giving the Broker the right to collect commission if the property is sold by anyone, including the owner, during the term of the agreement unless some specified exceptions to the agreement occur.

Exclusive Agency (ExclusiveAgency)

A contract giving one Brokerage Firm, for a specified time, the right to sell/lease the property and also allowing the owner, acting alone, to sell/lease the property without paying commission.

Co-Exclusive Agency (CoExclusiveAgency)

Cotality defines it only by name: “Co-Exclusive Agency”. The primary listing agent and the co-listing agent are separate fields on the listing; they can be in the same office, the same company or different companies, so the agreement says nothing about their offices.

Other Cotality values (Agent Search)

Open (Open)

Often used for commercial property, a listing given to any number of Brokers without liability to compensate any except the one who first secures a buyer who is ready, willing and able to meet the terms of the listing and secures the seller's acceptance. The seller may, acting alone, sell the property without paying commission.

Probate (Probate)

An Exclusive Right To Sell listing agreement that also resides under authority of the local probate code.

Net (Net)

A listing in which the broker's commission is the excess of the sale price over an agreed-upon (net0 price to the seller; illegal in some states because it can create a conflict of interest for the broker.

Non-Exclusive Agency, Property Management, Seller Reserved

Cotality defines each of these only by its name.
