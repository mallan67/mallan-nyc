Listing Type & Classification

Listing Type Guide (Sale)

Exclusive Right to Sell (ExclusiveRightToSell)

The broker earns commission no matter who sells the property — even if the owner finds the buyer themselves. This is the most common and most protective listing agreement.

Full RLS distribution. Full commission protection.

Exclusive Agency (ExclusiveAgency)

One broker is the exclusive agent, but if the owner sells the property themselves (no broker involved), they do NOT owe commission. The broker only earns commission if the sale goes through the brokerage.

RLS eligible, but less commission protection for the agent.

Exclusive Right w/ Exception (ExclusiveRightWithException)

Same as Exclusive Right to Sell, except for specific named exceptions listed in the agreement (e.g., "If buyer is John Smith, no commission owed"). Commission is owed on all other sales.

RLS eligible. Document the exceptions in the listing agreement.

Co-Exclusive (CoExclusive)

Two brokers share the exclusive right. Either broker can sell the property. Commission is owed regardless of who finds the buyer. The co-brokering terms must be in writing.

RLS eligible. Both firms named on listing.

In-House (Company Private) NOT ON RLS

A company-only private listing kept exclusively within Mallan Real Estate. Not submitted to REBNY RLS, not distributed to any portal. Only visible on mallan.nyc and marketed by our agents directly.

No RLS, no IDX, no syndication. Internal use only.

Owner Opt-Out (Permissions=OwnerOptOut)

Owner has signed Exhibit B opting out of public distribution. Listing goes to RLS but is hidden from all public channels (IDX, syndication, portals). Only visible to RLS Participants directly.

Participant Only (Permissions=Private)

Similar to Owner Opt-Out but set by the broker (not the owner). Listing is on RLS but not displayed publicly. Visible only to authorized RLS Participants.

Per UCBA: Only exclusive listings are eligible for RLS. The ListingAgreement field maps to RESO standard. In-House listings are internal only and are never submitted to REBNY.

Owner Opt-Out Selected

Per REBNY UCBA Art. I, Sec. 5(A): NO public dissemination whatsoever. All distribution channels will be disabled including IDX, Syndication, Listhub, NY MLS, Realtor, RPX, and WWW.

Allowed: One-to-one personal phone calls and individual emails to RLS Participants only.

Owner Opt-Out form (Exhibit B) must be signed and submitted via LMP within 48 hours.

Violation = Incurable: $250 first offense, $500 each subsequent.
