## Notes

- **Mallan-internal** columns are deliberately not RESO-aligned: things like `agent_id` (FK to our `Agent` model), `sync_status` (sync pipeline state), and the JSON columns (`address`, `features`, `media`, `compliance`, `agent_info`, `raw_data`) which carry RESO data inside them but are not 1:1 column-named.
- **REBNY-only** entries usually mean Trestle hasn't certified that field on the IDX Plus tier yet, or REBNY documents a field that Trestle exposes under a slightly different name.
- **Trestle-only** entries indicate fields the live feed exposes that REBNY hasn't formally licensed in the IDX Plus subset — useful but not contractual.
