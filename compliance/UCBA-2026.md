## Section A: New in 2026 — 8 Rules

### A.1 — Days on Market (DOM) Rules

| # | Rule | UCBA Ref | Description |
|---|------|----------|-------------|
| A1 | DOM Reset After 30 Days | Art. I, Sec. 11 | DOM resets to zero after 30 consecutive days in Withdrawn or Cancelled status |
| A2 | DOM Starts at RLS Transmission | Art. I, Sec. 11 | DOM starts when listing is transmitted to RLS, NOT listing contract date |
| A3 | DOM Resets on Close | Art. I, Sec. 11 | DOM resets on sold (closed) or rented (closed) |
| A4 | Coming Soon / Participant Only No DOM | Art. I, Sec. 11 | Coming Soon and Participant Only do NOT accrue DOM |
| A5 | No DOM Circumvention | Art. I, Sec. 11 | Cannot circumvent DOM by re-naming or re-listing |

| # | Rule | UCBA Ref | Description |
|---|------|----------|-------------|
| A6 | Protected Period — 6 Names | Art. II, Sec. 13 | Within 7 business days after listing expiration, Exclusive Broker delivers up to 6 names to Owner |
| A7 | Protected Period — 90 Days | Art. II, Sec. 13 | If contract/lease executed within 90 days with one of the 6 names, Exclusive Broker entitled to compensation |
| A8 | Owner Must Notify New Broker | Art. II, Sec. 13 | Owner must notify any New Exclusive Broker of the protected period provision |

## 6 Distribution Gates

### Gate 1: Owner Opt-Out (Art. I, Sec. 5(A))

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Owner signs Opt-Out Form (Exhibit B) | **ALL fields blocked from ALL channels.** No RLS, no IDX, no VOW, no Syndication, no public. |
| Exception | 1:1 personal phone calls and emails to Participants allowed. |
| Deadline | Form submitted through LMP within 48 hours. |

### Gate 2: Participant Only (Definition W)

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Listing flagged Participant Only | **All fields to RLS only.** No IDX, VOW, Syndication, websites, social media. |
| Constraint | Cannot combine with Owner Opt-Out. |

### Gate 3: IDX Display (Art. III, Sec. 2(C))

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| `InternetEntireListingDisplayYN = True` | All public-eligible fields flow to IDX broker websites. Attribution required. *(Trestle field; UCBA refers to "IDX Entire Listing Display" — no separate field on Trestle)* |
| `InternetEntireListingDisplayYN = False` | Excluded from all IDX. Remains on RLS. |

### Gate 4: Syndication (UCBA General)

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| `SyndicateTo` enabled | All public-eligible fields flow to opted-in third-party portals. *(Trestle field; UCBA references `SyndicateYN` as boolean)* |
| `SyndicateTo` disabled | Excluded from all syndication. Remains on RLS. |

### Gate 5: Coming Soon Status (Art. I, Sec. 16)

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Status = Coming Soon | Fields flow to RLS + display channels. Open Houses disabled. Showings restricted. Badge required. |

### Gate 6: Closed Status (Art. I, Sec. 6-7)

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Status = Closed | Closing Price + Closed Date REQUIRED within 24hrs. Buyer Agent now populated. Must remove/mark closed on website within 24hrs. |
