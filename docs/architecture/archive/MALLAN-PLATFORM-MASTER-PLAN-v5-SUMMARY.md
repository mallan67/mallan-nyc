**Status:** Superseded planning version preserved for historical comparison only.

- five business value streams;
- cross-cutting data, intelligence, workflow, governance and measurement fabrics;
- enterprise business capability systems;
- a dedicated search recovery program;
- intelligence embedded across search, leads, relationships, brokerage, clients, marketing, valuation, transactions and business analysis.

1. It treated broker and agent too separately rather than recognizing Maya as both representative broker and producing professional through one identity.

3. It scoped valuation too narrowly and did not fully define audience-specific advisory products for buyers, tenants, sellers, landlords, investors, bankers/lenders, attorneys/fiduciaries and internal strategy.
